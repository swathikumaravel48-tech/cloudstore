How it works

  1. Create your account. Sign up with your email. No card needed for the Free plan.
  2. Upload your files. Drag files or whole folders into your CloudStore.
  3. Access them anywhere. Sign in on any device and your files are there.
  4. Share when you want. Create a link, set an expiry date, and send it.

Questions

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. You get 5 GB with no payment details and no time limit.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Move up or down at any time. Your files stay where they are.

Where is this website hosted?

It is a static website. Its files are stored on GitHub Pages and served over the web.
